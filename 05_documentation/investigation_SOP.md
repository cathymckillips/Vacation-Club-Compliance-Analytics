# Standard operating procedure — compliance alert review

1. Verify alert rule, as-of date, member ID, and evidence reference.
2. Check record completeness and data freshness; quarantine invalid data.
3. Review relevant membership tier and authorized benefit/guest rules.
4. Reconstruct reservation or points history using source records.
5. Look for legitimate explanations and supporting approval evidence.
6. Assign priority and route to the appropriate Club/Legal/Operations owner if warranted.
7. Record finding: legitimate, confirmed violation, or inconclusive; document rationale.
8. Assign corrective action, owner, and due date; monitor to closure.
9. Review false-positive patterns and recommend rule changes subject to approval.

Privacy: use synthetic IDs only; do not put real member PII into published dashboards or repositories.
